# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Azra project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other community members.
- Use clear, inclusive language in issues, PRs, docs and chat.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without consent.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behaviour or repeated unwanted contact.
- Plagiarism or presenting others' work as your own.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove or
edit comments, commits, issues and PRs that do not align with this document.
If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately via
the repository's GitHub security advisory form or by contacting the repository
owner through GitHub. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed from their role.
